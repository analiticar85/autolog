AutoLog – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 September 2026

AutoLog is a car expense tracker for iPhone. It was designed so that your data never leaves your device. This page explains, in plain language, what the app does and does not do with your information.

What data the app stores

Everything you enter in AutoLog – vehicles, fuel fill-ups, services, expenses, reminders and the invoice photos or PDF files you attach – is stored only on your iPhone, inside the app's private storage. It is protected by your device passcode and by iOS encryption, and it is included in your normal iCloud or iTunes device backup if you have that enabled.

What we collect

Nothing. AutoLog has no user accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking. The app makes no network connections. We – the developer – have no access to your data and cannot see, recover or delete it.

Camera and photo library

AutoLog asks for camera and photo library access only when you choose to attach an invoice. The photo you select is copied into the app's private storage and used for nothing else. You can revoke this permission at any time in iOS Settings → AutoLog.

Text recognition

When you attach an invoice photo, the app can read numbers and dates from it to pre-fill the form. This recognition runs entirely on your device using Apple's built-in Vision framework. The photo is not sent anywhere.

Notifications

Reminders use local notifications scheduled on your device. No push-notification service is involved.

Export and backup

The export features (CSV, PDF, ZIP backup) create files on your device and hand them to the iOS share sheet. Where those files go afterwards – Files, iCloud Drive, AirDrop, e-mail – is entirely your choice and is governed by the privacy policy of the service you choose.

Deleting your data

Delete individual records inside the app, or delete the app itself to remove all of its data from your iPhone. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing further to delete on our side.

Children

AutoLog does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If a future version of AutoLog adds an optional feature that transmits data (for example cloud sync), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the feature will be off by default.
