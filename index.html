AutoLog – Support

AutoLog keeps the complete cost and maintenance history of your car on your iPhone: fuel, services (down to the part replaced), insurance, registration and other expenses – with the invoice attached, statistics, reminders, and export to CSV, PDF or a full backup.

Frequently asked

Where is my data? Only on your phone. Nothing is uploaded. See the privacy policy.

How do I move to a new phone? More → Export & backup → Full backup (ZIP), save it to Files or iCloud Drive. On the new phone, install AutoLog and choose Restore from backup.

How is fuel consumption calculated? Between two full-tank fill-ups, from the odometer difference and the litres added in between. Tick Full tank when you fill up completely; use I missed a previous fill-up if you forgot to log one.

The app didn't read my receipt. Text recognition is a helper that pre-fills fields; it works best with a flat, well-lit photo. You can always type the values yourself.

Reminders didn't fire. Check iOS Settings → AutoLog → Notifications is allowed. Mileage-based reminders show on the dashboard once you log an odometer reading close to the target.
